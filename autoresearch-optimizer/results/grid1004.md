# Model grid: Sonnet vs Opus vs Sopus on two benchmarks, 3 repeats each (2026-10-04)

`scripts/model_grid.sh grid1004`: 4 agents × 3 generations, local trusted evaluator, swarm seeds 0–2,
on `median_string` (four DNA instances of 280–688 bp + one 320 aa protein) and `median_string_long`
(four 1500 bp DNA + one 500 aa protein). Sopus is `--model sonnet,opus`: generation 1 on Sonnet,
generations 2–3 on Opus. 18 runs, about $27 of agent cost in total. Page with one quality-vs-efficiency
scatter per benchmark and the mean ± sd table: [grid1004.html](grid1004.html)
(`just autoresearch-viz grid artifacts/runs/grid1004-* -o results/grid1004.html`).

Mean ± standard deviation over 3 runs (gain relative to the seed solver):

| Benchmark | Config | Gain (validate) | Held-out gain | Agent cost | Wall clock | Points per dollar |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| median_string | Sonnet | +17.0% ± 1.0 | +16.7% ± 0.9 | **$0.84 ± 0.03** | **2.7 min ± 1.2** | **4,649** |
| median_string | Opus | **+18.5% ± 0.2** | **+18.3% ± 0.1** | $2.03 ± 0.12 | 3.7 min ± 0.2 | 2,078 |
| median_string | Sopus | +18.3% ± 0.5 | +17.8% ± 0.6 | $1.61 ± 0.07 | 3.1 min ± 0.3 | 2,594 |
| median_string_long | Sonnet | +26.4% ± 0.2 | +26.3% ± 0.3 | **$0.85 ± 0.06** | **2.2 min ± 0.2** | **9,233** |
| median_string_long | Opus | +27.0% ± 0.6 | +27.0% ± 0.9 | $2.02 ± 0.11 | 3.5 min ± 0.3 | 3,956 |
| median_string_long | Sopus | **+27.4% ± 0.3** | **+27.4% ± 0.2** | $1.67 ± 0.11 | 3.2 min ± 0.3 | 4,837 |

- **median_string**: Opus is the best on quality; Sopus is within about one standard deviation of it on
  validate (−0.2 points) and slightly behind on held-out (−0.5 points), for 21% less cost and 17% less
  time. Sonnet is the cheapest and fastest but 1.5 points behind, with the largest spread between repeats.
- **median_string_long**: Sopus is the best on quality, validate and held-out, and is cheaper and faster
  than Opus, so it dominates Opus there. Opus also varies more between repeats.
- On both benchmarks Sonnet and Sopus are on the Pareto front of the means; Opus is on it only on
  `median_string`, as the highest-quality point.
- Sonnet's wall-clock spread on `median_string` comes from one seed-1 generation where an agent ran for 171 s.
- With 3 generations, Sopus spends 2 of them on Opus, so its cost is closer to Opus than in the
  2-generation run ($0.89). A schedule that switches on stagnation would be the next thing to test.
